About Academic Relay

A Unified Academic Communication and Progress Platform

Real-time Portal Faculty & Students Progress Tracking Smart Search

Platform Overview

Discover what makes Academic Relay unique

Academic Relay is a real-time, web-based academic portal designed to streamline communication between faculty and students while enhancing accessibility to academic resources. Built using Python (Flask) and MySQL, it serves as a centralized hub where faculty can efficiently upload circulars, timetables, assignments, notes, and marks.

Core Features

Everything you need in one place

Faculty Dashboard

Upload circulars, assignments, timetables, marks, and notes department-wise with ease.

Student Dashboard

View content filtered by department, year, and section. Never miss an update.

Notes Download

Access and download subject-wise notes with just one click.

Smart Search

Find documents and updates quickly using intelligent keyword search.

Progress Reports

Visual performance tracking and analytics for better insights.

Secure Login

Role-based authentication for faculty and students.

Impact & Vision

Making education accessible for everyone

Academic Relay isn't just a college project — it's designed for real-world use, reducing dependency on third-party tools like WhatsApp or Google Drive for internal academic communication. It encourages structured dissemination of information and helps students stay organized and performance-aware. By centralizing all academic resources and communications, institutions can ensure that every student has equal access to materials and updates.
